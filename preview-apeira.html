apeira — italy's first international hacker house. the principle of the boundless.

we gather europe's most curious builders into a single house in milan and give them everything they need to do the most ambitious work of their lives.

watch the launch film →

the tour — a van across italy:

to launch the house we drove a van across italy, visiting 6 cities with hackathons, lectures and hackerspaces. we engaged 600+ builders and visited hubs like the apple developer academy in naples and the bending spoons offices.

600+ builders · 6 cities · ~20 events
the stops:
start · genova
cast off from genova — the van boarded the ferry and headed south to sicily.
01 · catania
fireside chat with sabrina maniscalco, ceo & founder of algorithmiq, italy's leading quantum-computing startup.
02 · napoli
hackerspace @ apple developer academy — builders shipped their own projects, closing with demos and a fireside chat on startups & fundraising.
03 · bologna
fireside chat with luca foresti, serial entrepreneur, now ceo of niulinx (autonomous driving for european roads).
04 · roma
hackathon — 100 builders, one day, a €50k prize pool, with codex and 8 italian startups.
05 · torino
lecture by alberto sangiovanni-vincentelli, pioneer of electronic design automation and co-founder of cadence & synopsys (~$200b combined).
06 · milano
fireside chat with valentina jerusalmi, general manager at bending spoons — scaling, acquisitions and operational execution. home of the house, the final stop.
